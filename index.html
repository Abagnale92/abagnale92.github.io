About Me

I am a computer scientist with a strong background in software engineering and IT management, specialized in project management and experienced in coordinating technical and creative projects.

Alongside my academic and professional path in computer science, I have developed a strong passion for game development, which led me to complete a Master’s program in Computer Game Development and specialized courses in Game Design and UI/UX for video games at Digital Bros Academy.

Today, I work at SPICI in Naples, where I combine technical, design, and management skills across different projects related to gamification, advergames, interactive experiences, immersive technologies, and digital innovation. My work involves not only game and software development, but also project coordination, stakeholder communication, documentation, technology scouting, and support in shaping new ideas for the cultural and creative industries.

I consider myself a versatile and curious professional. I enjoy exploring the full development process — from technical architecture and implementation to design, user experience, production, QA, and project organization. My goal is to keep building a hybrid profile that allows me to connect people, ideas, technologies, and processes, contributing effectively across both creative and technical contexts.
